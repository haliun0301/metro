# Nickname image trials — v1

Six proposed interpretations for review before image generation. These images symbolize nicknames; they do not depict verified personal appearance or biography.

## Shared style

Editorial still-life and landscape photography with natural material textures, restrained saturation, warm cream highlights, muted blue and sage accents, gentle contrast, and subtle fine film grain. Soft daylight, simple composition, one dominant focal subject, generous negative space. Quiet observational mood matching a photographic magazine collage. Square image with the main subject within the central 60 percent, legible as a small thumbnail and suitable for a circular crop. No text, letters, logos, watermark, collage, borders, illustration, or 3D-rendered appearance.

## 花生 — Peanut

Direct literal reading: an unshelled peanut.

Photograph one unshelled peanut and one exposed kernel resting close together on warm ivory paper. Close view showing the delicate ridges of the shell. Soft light from the upper left and a short gentle shadow. Keep the peanut large and centrally placed; no bowl or additional props.

## Coffee — Coffee

Direct literal reading: a cup of coffee.

Photograph a small unbranded cream ceramic cup filled with dark coffee on a matte beige tabletop, viewed at a gentle angle from above. The coffee surface and cup rim are clearly visible. Diffused window light from the left, subtle shadow, ample empty background. No hands, saucer, beans, or decorative props.

## flying-fish — Flying fish

Literal visual interpretation of the English nickname.

Create a photographic image of a single silver-blue flying fish gliding just above a calm muted blue sea, its wing-like pectoral fins extended. Side view with the whole fish clearly visible and centrally framed. Pale softly blurred horizon, diffused morning daylight, restrained motion. Anatomically plausible fish fins, no bird wings, no extra fish, no dramatic splash.

## 回忆的天空 — Sky of memories

Poetic interpretation: a quiet sky with a nostalgic photographic treatment; not a claim about personal memories.

Photograph a pale blue sky with one large soft white cloud centered above a very thin strip of distant rooftops along the bottom edge. Gentle late-afternoon light, slightly faded photographic colors and fine grain evoke a remembered summer day. The sky dominates the image. No people, signage, landmark emphasis, or heavy vintage effects.

## 大花 — Big flower

Literal reading of the nickname: one large bloom; the flower species is an artistic choice.

Photograph one large dusty-pink dahlia bloom on a short visible green stem against a plain warm cream background. Face the flower toward the camera, with the entire bloom inside the central area and all petals visible. Soft side daylight reveals the petal texture. One flower only, no vase, bouquet, or decorative pattern.

## 小朦胧 — A little hazy

Atmospheric interpretation of 朦胧: soft mist and partial visibility.

Photograph a single small leafy tree emerging from light morning mist beside still water. Center the tree and its faint reflection against a pale gray-green background, with no other distinct trees. Diffused light, gently softened distant edges, but enough definition in the main silhouette to read at thumbnail size. Calm restrained atmosphere, no people or buildings.
